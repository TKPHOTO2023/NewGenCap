Tool 01

Funding Readiness Assessment

Answer ten short questions about your project. You will get a readiness score, a breakdown by area and a list of what to prepare before funders will engage.

  • About 3 minutes
  • No sign-up needed to see your score
  • Optional PDF report to share

Question 1 of 10

What stage is your project at?
Do you have a business plan and financial model?
How certain is the project’s income?

For example offtake agreements, leases or customer contracts.

How much of the project cost will you contribute?

Your own equity, in cash or assets.

What is your team’s track record?
How far is the technical work?
Are land, permits and licences in place?
What financial records does the company have?
Have you assessed the project’s risks?
How clear is your funding requirement?

    Tool 02

    Telecoms Readiness Assessment

    For fibre, tower, wireless and data centre projects. Ten questions on the things funders check in a network build: design and build cost, rights and licences, demand and operations.

    • About 3 minutes
    • Built from our directors’ network delivery experience
    • Optional PDF report to share

    Question 1 of 10

    How firm are the network design and build cost?
    Have equipment vendors and supply been secured?

    For example fibre, active equipment, towers, power and cooling.

    Is there a phased rollout plan with milestones?
    Are site access, wayleaves and rights of way secured?
    Does the project hold the ICASA licences it needs?
    Are municipal, environmental and building approvals in place?

    For example trenching permits and tower site approvals.

    How proven is demand on the planned route or sites?
    Are there anchor tenants or wholesale agreements?

    For example ISPs, mobile operators, municipalities or enterprise clients.

    What is your team’s network build track record?
    How will the network be operated and maintained?
