What it means for our clients

Delivering a network at this scale shows what it takes to build digital infrastructure in South Africa: wayleaves, phased rollouts, contractor management and the gap between planned and actual build costs. We bring that experience to every project we assess. For capital providers, it means build costs checked against the design and quotes, and progress verified on site before each drawdown.
