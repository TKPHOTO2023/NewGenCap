About us

The full namebehind hostdigi.

Many clients meet us through our hosting platform. The rest of the house is why they stay.

Why one house

Six suppliers. Nobody owned the result.

Growing online used to take a hosting company, a web agency, a production house, a media buyer, a marketing retainer and, lately, someone who understands AI.

Each did their part well enough. When leads dried up, each could point at someone else. HostDigital exists to end that: every discipline under one roof, with one lead who answers for the outcome.

It changes the work. The people planning your campaign can see your server's load times. The people building your AI agent know what your sales team says on the phone. The studio shoots content for the placements the media team has already negotiated.

We build our own software for the same reason. Tenderiza, InstantDM and hostdigi started as tools we needed for client work. Today they're products in their own right, and every client benefits from what we learn running them.

What we hold ourselves to

Three rules we don't bend.

Accountable end to end

One lead owns your account across every service. If something underperforms, you hear it from us first, with the fix.

Measured in revenue

Reach and engagement are inputs. We report on enquiries, qualified leads, deals and what each one cost.

Built, not resold

Our hosting, our studio, our AI products. Fewer middlemen means faster changes and fewer surprises.
