Our process

From origination to funding — one disciplined process.

Many opportunities come in. Only deals that are credible, commercially viable and deliverable pass the decision gate and are presented for funding.

Decision gate
  1. 01

    Find

    Source clients who need funding for viable projects.

  2. 02

    Screen

    Assess credibility, scalability and business potential.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Establish commercial viability and the investment case.

  4. 04

    Package

    Structure a funder-ready deal and present it for funding.

  5. 05

    Fund

    The capital provider funds approved projects.

    Funder’s role
  6. 06

    Deliver

    Monitor and project-manage to profitable completion.

How capital is protected after funding

  • Screening gate

    Standard credibility and viability criteria applied before any deal is presented.

  • Milestone drawdowns

    Funds released against verified project progress, not upfront.

  • Monthly reporting

    Progress, spend against plan, risks and early warnings.

  • Independent monitoring

    We oversee delivery on the ground through to completion.

We earn when a deal funds and when it is delivered well — our incentives mirror the funder’s own.
